The Start-to-End Procedure in Sri Lanka

A comprehensive, phase-by-phase breakdown of the official licensing procedure administered by the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) and National Transport Medical Institute (NTMI).

1

Medical Fitness Examination (NTMI)

National Transport Medical Institute Physical & Optical Certification
Mandatory Step Fee: ~Rs. 1,750 Valid for 6 Months

What is the NTMI Medical Examination?

Before you can register at the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT), you must obtain an official Medical Fitness Certificate from the National Transport Medical Institute (NTMI). Medical examinations from private general practitioners or ordinary clinics are not accepted by the DMT.

Booking Your Appointment

  • Online Booking: Book via the eChannelling platform or the official NTMI appointment portal.
  • Telephone Hotline: Dial 225 (from any mobile network) or Government Information Center 1919 to reserve a time slot.
  • NTMI Centers: Available at Werahera (Head Office), Nugegoda, Gampaha, Kandy, Kurunegala, Galle, Anuradhapura, Kalutara, Badulla, Hambantota, and all district branches.

Medical Tests Conducted on Exam Day

  • Visual Acuity (Eye Test): Reading the Snellen distance vision chart with each eye independently. Both naked eyesight and corrected vision (with spectacles) are tested.
  • Color Vision: Identification of numbers on Ishihara color plates to ensure the applicant can distinguish traffic lights (Red, Amber, Green).
  • Hearing & Balance: Basic audiometry and motor reflex coordination checks.
  • Blood Pressure & Physical Reflexes: Checking resting blood pressure, limb mobility, grip strength, and spinal movement.
  • Urine / Blood Sugar Test: Fasting blood glucose or urine dipstick check to rule out uncontrolled diabetes or organ conditions.
  • ECG (Electrocardiogram): Mandatory for candidates aged 50 and above or those applying for heavy commercial vehicle licenses.
💡 Pro Tip: If you wear corrective glasses or contact lenses for distant vision, wear them to the examination. Your certificate will simply be marked with the condition "Valid only when wearing corrective lenses", which will also be noted on your final driving license card.
Minimum Age 17 Years (To sit medical & apply for L-Permit)
Documents to Bring Original NIC / Passport + Spectacles + Studio photos
Certificate Validity Strictly 6 Months from date of issue
Key Outcome NTMI Form No. 1 (Fitness Certificate)
⚠️ Warning: Arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time. Bring cash for payment as card terminals may experience network downtime.
2

DMT Registration & Biometrics Enrollment

Department of Motor Traffic (Werahera / District Branch)
Document Submission Gov Fee: ~Rs. 2,500 - 3,200

Registering at the DMT

Once you hold a valid NTMI Medical Certificate, you proceed to the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT). For Western Province, the primary licensing center is at Werahera (Boralesgamuwa). Outside the Western Province, registration takes place at your respective District Secretariat DMT office.

Step-by-Step Registration Procedure

  • DMT Appointment Portal: Pre-book your visiting date and time slot via the DMT appointment website (dmt.gov.lk) to receive an electronic appointment token.
  • Obtaining Form MTA 6: Collect and fill Application Form MTA 6 (available in Sinhala, Tamil, or English). Enter your personal details exactly as they appear on your National Identity Card.
  • Document Verification (Counter 1): Present your original NIC, original Birth Certificate, original NTMI medical form, and 2 passport photos with barcode. The verifying officer stamps and approves your application.
  • Biometrics Capture (Photo & Fingerprint): Move to the digital biometrics booth where your ten fingerprints are scanned, facial photo is captured for the digital database, and your signature is taken on an electronic pad.
  • Payment of Government Fee: Pay the official government license registration and theory exam fee at the cashier counters and obtain your official receipt.
📌 Important Studio Photo Rule: The DMT requires photographs taken only at studios authorized by the Department of Motor Traffic. These photos feature a unique digital barcode printed at the bottom edge that links directly to the DMT central database.
Primary Center DMT Head Office, Werahera, Boralesgamuwa
District Centers Kandy, Kurunegala, Anuradhapura, Galle, etc.
Form Required MTA 6 (Application for New Driving Licence)
Biometrics Digital facial photo + Fingerprints + Signature
⚠️ Never Pay Touts: Unauthorized agents outside the gates cannot expedite your application or alter exam results. All official transactions are strictly processed inside through government counters.
3

The Theory Written Examination

Computerized Touchscreen Testing Terminal / Paper MCQ Test
40 Questions Time: 60 Minutes Pass Mark: 30 / 40 (75%)

The Official DMT Written Test Format

At the Werahera main office, the theory examination is conducted on modern touchscreen computerized terminals in a secure exam hall. In some district centers, it is conducted using printed multiple-choice question sheets. You can choose to take the exam in English, Sinhala, or Tamil.

Question Distribution & Structure

  • Road Signs & Markings (approx. 15 questions): Danger Warning Signs, Prohibitory, Mandatory, Priority signs, road centerlines, yellow box junctions, and pedestrian crossings.
  • Rules of the Road & Priority (approx. 12 questions): Right-of-way at roundabouts, overtaking regulations, turning maneuvers, emergency vehicles, and lane discipline.
  • Speed Limits & Expressways (approx. 5 questions): Urban 50 km/h, rural 70 km/h, expressway 100 km/h max / 60 km/h min, and vehicle restrictions.
  • Vehicle Safety & Basic Mechanics (approx. 5 questions): Tire tread depth (min 1.6mm), ABS brakes, emergency blowouts, dashboard warning lights, and pre-drive checks.
  • Legal Obligations & Penalties (approx. 3 questions): Blood alcohol limit (0.06%), accidents, document inspection, and learner driver rules.

Instant Results & Re-sitting Rules

On computerized terminals, as soon as you finish question 40 and click submit, your final score is instantly displayed. If you score 30 or more (≥ 75%), you receive your official passing slip immediately. If you fail (score below 30), you may re-register for the written test by paying a nominal re-sit fee of approximately Rs. 500 after a short waiting period.

4

Learner's Permit (L-Permit) & 3-Month Training

Mandatory 90-Day Learning Period on Public Roads
Form MTA 3 Validity: 18 Months Mandatory 3 Months Waiting

Receiving Your Learner's Permit

Upon passing the written examination, the DMT immediately issues your official Learner's Permit (Form MTA 3). This permit is valid for 18 months, giving you ample time to practice driving and prepare for the practical test.

The Mandatory 3-Month Waiting Period Rule

Under Sri Lankan road traffic law, a learner MUST practice for a minimum of 3 full months (90 days) from the date of issuance of the learner's permit before they are legally permitted to appear for the practical driving trial. This mandatory period ensures candidates gain real-world behind-the-wheel road experience.

Strict Legal Conditions for Learner Drivers

  • Red "L" Plate Requirement: You must display an official red capital letter "L" on a white rectangular reflective board on both the front and rear of the vehicle, clearly visible from at least 30 meters.
  • Accompanying Driver Rule: You must never drive alone. You must always be accompanied by a licensed driving instructor or a person holding a valid Sri Lankan driving license for the same vehicle class for at least 3 years, seated in the front passenger seat.
  • Expressway Ban: Learner drivers are strictly prohibited from driving on all Expressways (E01, E02, E03, E04). Entering an expressway with an L-permit is a severe statutory offense.
  • Peak Hour Restrictions: In congested urban centers like Colombo, learner vehicles are prohibited from practicing on designated central arteries during morning and evening rush hours.

Driving School vs Private Practice

Driving School (Recommended): Enrolling in a registered driving school (Learners) provides dual-control training cars, professional instructors who know the examiner routes, mock trials on the Werahera track, and guaranteed vehicle hire on trial day.
Private Practice: You can practice in your personal family vehicle provided you have an accompanying driver licensed for 3+ years and display proper "L" plates. However, you will need to rent an approved dual-pedal car for the practical trial day.

Document Issued Learner's Driving Permit (MTA 3)
Total Validity 18 Months from date of issue
Minimum Training Gap 90 Days (3 Calendar Months)
Accompanying Driver Must hold valid class license for ≥ 3 years
🚫 Heavy Fine: Driving on public roads without an accompanying qualified driver or without displaying "L" plates carries immediate vehicle impoundment and cancellation of your learner permit.
5

The Practical Driving Trial

Ground Maneuvers Track Test + Real Road Traffic Driving Test
Age 18+ Required Trial Track + Road Test Motor Vehicle Examiner (MVE)

Eligibility & Booking Your Trial Date

To face the practical driving trial, you must have completed the 3-month waiting period and be at least 18 years old. You visit the DMT office with your learner permit to book your trial appointment date on the official test track.

Part A: Ground / Track Maneuvers Test

🅿️

1. The Reverse "Box" Parking

Reversing the vehicle into a marked rectangular bay between poles without hitting any pole or kerb.

  • ✓ Check all mirrors and rear window before moving
  • ✓ Reverse smoothly without stopping abruptly
  • ✓ Must not touch or graze any marker pole
🔄

2. The Three-Point Turn ("Z" Turn)

Turning the vehicle around to face the opposite direction in a narrow street between boundaries.

  • ✓ Indicate right before pulling across
  • ✓ Look both ways before reversing
  • ✓ Do not mount or hit the kerb/boundary
⛰️

3. The Hill Start (Incline Test)

Stopping on a steep ramp, securing with handbrake, and moving off smoothly in 1st gear.

  • ✓ Apply handbrake firmly when stopped
  • ✓ Find biting point on clutch before releasing handbrake
  • ✓ Must not roll backwards more than 6 inches
🏍️

4. Motorcycle "Figure of 8"

Continuous figure-eight balance ride for motorcycle candidates (Class A/A1).

  • ✓ Complete full 8 pattern inside lines
  • ✓ Strictly forbidden to put foot on ground
  • ✓ Maintain smooth throttle and balance

Part B: Real Road Driving Test

After clearing the track maneuvers, the candidate drives on public city roads with an official Motor Vehicle Examiner (MVE) sitting in the passenger seat. The examiner evaluates:

  • Cockpit Drill: Adjusting seat, checking rear-view and side mirrors, fastening seatbelt, verifying handbrake is engaged before starting.
  • Moving Off & Blind Spots: Checking interior mirror, right side mirror, and looking over right shoulder (blind spot) before indicating and moving into traffic.
  • Lane Discipline & Positioning: Keeping to the left lane under normal driving, not straddling continuous center white lines.
  • Roundabouts & Junctions: Giving way to vehicles on the right at roundabouts; indicating at least 30 meters before turns.
  • Pedestrian Courtesy: Stopping completely before pedestrian crossings when pedestrians are waiting to cross.
  • Speed Control & Braking: Driving smoothly within the speed limit, avoiding jerky braking, maintaining the 2-second following distance.

The 8 Critical Instant-Fail Faults (The "Golden Don'ts")

  1. Touching or knocking over any pole during reverse parking or 3-point turn.
  2. Putting your foot on the ground during the motorcycle figure-8 maneuver.
  3. Rolling backwards on the hill start incline test.
  4. Failing to stop at a STOP sign or zebra crossing with pedestrians present.
  5. Failing to give way at a roundabout or junction, causing another vehicle to brake or swerve.
  6. Failing to check blind spots over your shoulder before pulling out or changing lanes.
  7. Overtaking across solid or double continuous white centerlines.
  8. Stalling the engine repeatedly (3 times or more) during the road test.
Testing Official Motor Vehicle Examiner (MVE) - DMT
Vehicle Required Dual-pedal control vehicle with revenue license & insurance
Test Components 1. Ground Maneuvers + 2. Road Traffic Drive
If You Fail Re-trial booking available after 30 days
💡 Mindset Tip: Examiners want to see calm, safe, defensive driving. Keep both hands on the steering wheel (ten-to-two or quarter-to-three position), check your mirrors every 10-15 seconds, and always signal early!
6

Temporary Permit & Smart Card License Issuance

Receiving Your Official Sri Lankan Driving License
Trial Passed! Temporary Permit: 6 Months Smart Card Validity: 8 Years

Congratulations, You Passed! What Happens Next?

When you successfully pass both the track maneuvers and the road driving test, the Motor Vehicle Examiner signs and stamps your official trial evaluation card.

Issuance of the Temporary Driving Permit

You take your signed trial card to the DMT administrative counter. The officer issues you an official Temporary Driving Permit (Paper License) immediately. This temporary document is valid for 6 months and allows you to legally drive on all public roads in Sri Lanka as a fully licensed driver without "L" plates and without needing an accompanying driver.

The Biometric Smart Card Driving License

Your permanent high-security plastic Smart Card Driving License features:

  • High-resolution laser-engraved facial photograph and electronic signature.
  • Digital microprocessor chip containing your biometric fingerprints and driving record.
  • Official vehicle category classes stamped on the reverse side (e.g. Class B for Light Motor Car, Class A for Motorcycle).
  • Any driving restriction codes (e.g., Code 01 for Corrective Lenses, Code 78 for Automatic Transmission Only).

Card Collection & Delivery

  • Registered Post Delivery: The card is printed and dispatched to your registered residential address via Sri Lanka Post Registered Mail within 30 to 45 working days.
  • Same-Day One-Day Service: Available at Werahera for an additional expedited government fee for urgent overseas travel or employment needs.

Validity & Demerit Points System

The standard light vehicle license is valid for 8 years. Drivers must renew their license upon expiry by submitting a fresh NTMI medical fitness certificate. Sri Lanka operates a driver demerit points system where committing critical traffic offenses accumulates penalty points that can lead to court summons or license suspension.

Immediate Document Temporary Driving Permit (Valid 6 Months)
Permanent Document Biometric Smart Card Driving License
Delivery Time 30 - 45 Days via Registered Post
License Validity 8 Years (Renewable with NTMI Medical)
🚗 Always Remember: Carry your driving license, vehicle revenue license, insurance certificate, and emission certificate whenever you drive. Safe travels on Sri Lankan roads!
